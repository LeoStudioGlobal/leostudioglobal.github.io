Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to LeoStudioGlobal. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile games (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Our Service is designed to be fully playable without requiring you to create an account on our servers. However, to provide essential features and keep the Service free, we integrate with industry-standard third-party services that may collect specific data:

  • Advertising (AppLovin MAX & Google AdMob): To provide free gameplay, we display personalized and non-personalized ads. Our advertising partners may collect your IP address, device identifiers (such as Apple IDFA or Google AAID), and general location data.
  • Analytics (Firebase Analytics & Unity Engine): We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Unity Telemetry to understand how players interact with our game (e.g., level completion rates, crash reports). This data is anonymized and used exclusively to improve game performance and balance.
  • Cloud Save (Apple Game Center & Google Play Games): To allow you to sync your progress across devices, we use native OS game services. Your save data (e.g., levels unlocked) is securely stored in Apple's or Google's cloud ecosystem. We do not collect, process, or store your personal names, passwords, or emails on our own servers.
  • Native Share: When you use the share feature in our game, it invokes your device's native sharing UI. Our game code does not and cannot access your contacts, photos, or social media accounts.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to delete it.

3. California Privacy Rights (CCPA Compliance)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you specific rights regarding your personal information. We do not sell your personal information. We only share anonymized identifiers with our advertising partners to serve ads. You have the right to request what data is being collected and request its deletion by contacting us.

4. European Union Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase any personal data we may hold, and to restrict or object to its processing. You will be prompted with a consent dialog for targeted advertising upon launching the app if required by your region.
